How to play

Aim & shoot: aim the guide line and shoot a bubble toward the board.

Match 3: connect three or more bubbles of the same color to pop them.

Plan your shots: drop larger clusters and use boosters when a level becomes difficult.

Earn stars: complete each puzzle efficiently and continue your journey across the map.
